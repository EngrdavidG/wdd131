School Management System - Site Plan

Site Name

SmartSchool Manager

This name represents a modern digital platform designed to manage school activities efficiently. It reflects innovation, organization, and smart handling of school data.

Optional Domain: smartschoolmanager.com

Site Purpose

The School Management System is designed to provide a centralized platform for managing school operations. It will allow users to create, read, update, and delete (CRUD) essential school data such as student records, staff information, and academic content.

The website will include:

  • Home page with general school information
  • About Us page describing the school and its history
  • Contact page with a form for communication

The system will be built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with future expansion into a full real-world application.

Scenarios

  • How can I register a new student into the system?
  • Where can I find information about the school’s history and mission?
  • How do I contact the school administration?

Color Schema

The website will use a clean and professional color scheme:

  • Purple: Used for header, footer, and highlights
  • White: Used for background and text contrast

Typography

  • Poppins: Used for headings
  • Roboto: Used for body text
